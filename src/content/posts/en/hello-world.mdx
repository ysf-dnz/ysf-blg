For years my notes have been scattered: NotebookLM notebooks, half-finished
drafts, browser tabs. This blog is an attempt to turn that mess into output.

I will write about artificial intelligence, software, entrepreneurship and
education. At the end of each post you will see the sources it draws on —
they usually lead to a notebook in
[my library](https://ysf-dnz.github.io/kutuphanem).
